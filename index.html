About this atlasReal sky, open data

The sky dome is computed locally from your chosen place and UTC time. At narrow fields, the view blends into real telescope survey imagery.

  • CDS HiPS2FITS — survey delivery
  • DSS2, Pan-STARRS DR1, and 2MASS — sky imagery
  • Yale Bright Star Catalog, SIMBAD, and OpenNGC — curated object data
  • NASA, ESA, ESO, and Wikimedia Commons — credited object photography

Survey and object-image licenses belong to their source archives. Atlas code is MIT licensed.
